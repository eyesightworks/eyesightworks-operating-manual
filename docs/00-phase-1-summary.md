# Objective Achieved

Phase 1 established the identity, direction, governance, and operating philosophy of EyesightWorks Technologies.

The objective was not to design software, but to create a decision-ready operating manual that aligns future business, product, architecture, and engineering work.

---

# Key Decisions

The following strategic decisions have been approved.

## Company Identity

* Company Name: **EyesightWorks Technologies**
* Tagline: **Building Africa's Business Operating System**
* North Star: **Help African businesses operate better using software and AI.**

---

## Strategic Direction

The company exists to build practical software that helps businesses:

* Save time
* Improve decision-making
* Automate repetitive work
* Grow sustainably

Growth will be driven by solving real customer problems rather than building large numbers of features.

---

## Strategic Focus

EyesightWorks Technologies follows a **depth before breadth** strategy.

The company will validate one product at a time while continuously investing in reusable platform capabilities that support future products.

Expansion follows evidence rather than assumptions.

---

## Four Operating Pillars

Every important decision should strengthen one or more of these pillars:

* Customer Value
* Business Sustainability
* Platform Scalability
* Execution Speed

These pillars guide prioritization across the company.

---

## Development Philosophy

Business decisions guide product decisions.

Product decisions guide architecture.

Architecture guides engineering.

Technology exists to support business outcomes.

---

## Governance Principles

The company adopts the following governance principles:

* Documentation accelerates execution.
* Governance serves execution, not bureaucracy.
* Important decisions are documented and traceable.
* Approved documents remain stable until new evidence justifies change.
* Learning through customer validation is preferred over internal assumptions.

---

## Documentation Strategy

The operating manual exists to improve decision quality, execution speed, and organizational clarity.

Every document should help answer important business or engineering questions.

If documentation no longer improves decisions, it should be simplified.

---

## Decision Framework

Every significant decision will include:

* Decision ID
* Context
* Decision
* Reasoning
* Consequences
* Affected Documents
* Review Trigger
* Owner
* Date

Decision categories:

* BUS — Business
* PROD — Product
* ARCH — Architecture
* ENG — Engineering
* OPS — Operations

---

## Company Operating Rhythm

The company follows a weekly execution cycle supported by a **Weekly Evidence Review**.

Each review answers five questions:

1. What did we build?
2. What did we learn?
3. Which assumptions changed?
4. What blocked progress?
5. What is the next most important objective?

The purpose is continuous improvement through evidence.

# Transition to Phase 2

With the company foundation complete, attention shifts from defining **who we are** to validating **how the business will create value**.

Phase 2 focuses on designing the smallest evidence-driven business model capable of reaching the company's next milestone.

## Phase 2 Objective

Design a business architecture that clearly defines:

* Customer segments
* Beachhead market
* Value propositions
* Revenue model
* Customer acquisition
* Customer retention
* Validation experiments
* Business assumptions
* Success metrics

Every major assumption should include a validation plan before influencing architecture or engineering decisions.

---

# Success Criteria for Phase 2

Phase 2 will be considered successful when a reader can clearly understand:

* How EyesightWorks creates value.
* Who the first customers are.
* Why customers would choose the product.
* How revenue will be generated.
* Which assumptions require validation.
* How customer evidence will guide future decisions.
